How Kurinda works

A clearer picture, from orbit to ground.

Satellite Earth Observation

Indicators of hydrology, vegetation and land-surface change using satellite data.

Ground Sensors

Low-cost ground-based sensors measuring soil moisture, ground movement and temperature.

Machine Learning

Pattern analysis helps combine satellite and ground sensor data into clear risk predictions.

Early Warning System

Data driven warning signals to alert at risk communities.
