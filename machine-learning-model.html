Machine Learning Model

A machine learning model integrates the EO and ground sensor data to produce landslide and flood risk analysis and mapping.

Overview

The model brings satellite observations and live ground readings together in one analysis. Its output is landslide and flood risk mapping that supports locally useful decisions and the alerts shown on the Alerts page.

How it fits in

  • Inputs: Earth Observation data and ground sensor readings
  • Outputs: landslide and flood risk analysis and maps
  • Feeds the project's early warning alerts
